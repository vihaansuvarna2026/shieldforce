The engine needs every phase to succeed. Refusing one OTP, one QR scan or one 'fee' collapses the whole chain — you don't need to win six battles, just one.

⏱ The clock runs both ways

Scammers use urgency against you; the Golden Window is urgency working for you. The faster the 1930 call after extraction, the higher the freeze chances.

📣 Reporting is ammunition

Phases 1 and 6 feed on silence. Every NCRP report — even of a failed attempt — burns numbers, handles and mule accounts the network needs.
