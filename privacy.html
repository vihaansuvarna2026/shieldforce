Privacy Policy

Short version: Shield Force does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Everything you type stays on your device.

Last updated: 20 September 2026

1. Who we are

Shield Force is a community awareness initiative that helps Indian Armed Forces personnel, veterans and their families recognise and avoid financial fraud. It is not an official Government of India or Ministry of Defence service. Questions about this policy can be sent to vihaanssuvarna@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. Shield Force has no backend server, no user accounts and no analytics or advertising software of any kind. The app runs entirely as code inside your browser or the installed app shell on your device.

3. The Shield AI message scanner

When you paste a message into the Shield AI Analyzer, that text is scored entirely by JavaScript running on your own device against a local list of fraud patterns. The message is never uploaded, logged or sent to any server — it is discarded the moment you navigate away or clear the field.

4. What stays on your device

A small amount of information is saved using your browser's local storage, purely so the app remembers your progress between visits. This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us:

  • Training scores — your best result on each quiz module, so progress isn't lost.
  • Interface preferences — things like whether you've dismissed a banner.

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing site data / app storage for Shield Force in your browser or device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

5. Offline support & caching

Shield Force installs a service worker so pages, images and the downloadable PDF reports keep working without an internet connection after your first visit. This is a standard web technology for offline apps and does not collect or transmit any data about you.

6. Third-party links

Pages such as Emergency and the footer link out to official government resources — cybercrime.gov.in, Sanchar Saathi, SPARSH and KSB — and to the national helpline via tel:1930. Those destinations are outside our control and have their own privacy practices.

7. Permissions

Shield Force does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos or notifications. The only device capability it uses is local storage, described in section 4.

8. Children's privacy

Shield Force is educational content aimed at adult service members, veterans and family members. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, so no age-specific handling is required — nothing is collected from any visitor regardless of age.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example if a future version adds optional analytics — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data-deletion requests, or anything else: email vihaanssuvarna@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Use & Disclaimer.
