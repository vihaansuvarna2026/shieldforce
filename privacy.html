Privacy Policy

Short version: Shield Force has no database and no accounts. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data, and everything you type stays on your device.

This is a translation provided for your convenience. If it differs from the English version, the English version applies.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy applies to the Shield Force app for Android (Google Play, package in.shieldforce.app), the Shield Force app for iPhone and iPad (App Store), and the Shield Force website. It explains what happens to information about you and your device when you use any of them. The short answer is that nothing is collected, nothing is shared and nothing leaves your device.

1. Who we are

Shield Force is a community awareness initiative that helps Indian Armed Forces personnel, veterans and their families recognise and avoid financial fraud. It is developed and published by the independent Shield Force developer shown on the app's store page, and it is not an official Government of India or Ministry of Defence service. Privacy contact: vihaanssuvarna@gmail.com (see also our Contact page).

2. Data we collect

None. Shield Force does not collect, receive, store on a server, or transmit any personal or sensitive information about you. It has no backend server, no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software of any kind. The app runs entirely as code on your device.

3. There is no database

Shield Force does not operate a database, and does not need one. The app is a set of static files. Nothing you enter is ever sent to a server, there is no place where user records could be written, and no account system to create a record in the first place. We hold no user table, no message log, no quiz history and no contact list.

You never register, sign in, or provide a name, phone number, email address, service number, PPO number, rank, unit or bank detail anywhere in this app — nothing asks for them, because there is nowhere to put them.

This has a practical consequence worth stating plainly: there is no store of user data here that could be breached, sold, subpoenaed, or leaked, because it does not exist. We cannot hand over information about you to anyone, including ourselves.

4. Device information and identifiers

Shield Force does not read, collect or use any device identifier — not the IMEI, IMSI, SIM serial number, phone number, Android ID, advertising ID (AAID/IDFA), MAC address or any other identifier — and does not fingerprint your device. It does not access your location, contacts, call logs, SMS, photos, camera or microphone. Because it collects no identifiers and no personal data, it never links one to another.

5. The message scanner (“Shield AI”)

When you paste a message into the message scanner, that text is checked entirely on your own device against a built-in list of fraud patterns. The scanner is a set of rules inside the app; it does not use any third-party or online AI service. The message is never uploaded, logged, stored or sent anywhere — it is discarded the moment you leave the screen or clear the field.

6. What stays on your device

A small amount of information is saved in the app's local storage on your device, purely so the app remembers your progress and settings between visits. It is never sent to us or anyone else and is not visible to us. The only copy that can exist elsewhere is inside your phone's own encrypted device backup (iCloud or Google), if you have backups switched on:

  • Training scores — your best result on each quiz module, so progress isn't lost.
  • App settings — such as that you have accepted the terms, your language choice, and whether you've dismissed a banner.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, trade or disclose any user data to anyone — no advertisers, data brokers, analytics or AI companies, partners or government bodies — because we do not have any. There are no categories of third parties with whom we share user data.

8. Third-party code in the app

The store apps are built with Capacitor, an open-source app framework, together with the standard Android and Apple system components. None of this code collects, stores or sends data about you or your device. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, social-login, crash-reporting or AI software development kits (SDKs).

9. Internet use and permissions

Everything in the app — every screen, the scanner and the training — works offline from the first launch. The app's own code makes no network requests. The Android app includes the standard internet permission that Android's built-in web view requires; it is used only when you choose to open an outside link, such as a government website or one of the PDF field guides, which then open in your phone's browser.

Shield Force does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, files or notifications. The only device capability it uses is local storage, described in section 6.

10. Offline support & caching (website)

On the website, Shield Force installs a service worker so pages, images and the downloadable PDF reports keep working without an internet connection after your first visit. This is a standard web technology for offline apps and does not collect or transmit any data about you.

11. Hosting and app stores

The app-store versions of Shield Force carry every screen inside the app on your device, so nothing about you is sent anywhere when you use them.

If you use Shield Force as a website, the company that hosts it (Netlify) receives the standard technical details that every website visit involves — such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested — in order to deliver the pages and protect against abuse. Those logs are held by the host under its own privacy policy; Shield Force does not use them to identify or track anyone.

Google and Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated statistics such as total downloads. These are produced by the stores under their own privacy policies, not by the app, and contain no personal data.

12. Security

The best protection for personal data is not to collect it, which is how Shield Force is built. What the app saves stays in its private storage on your device, protected by your phone's own security (screen lock and encryption). The website is served only over encrypted HTTPS.

13. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side and no account to close. The settings and scores on your device stay there until you remove them: clear your quiz scores with More → Reset training progress, or erase everything by clearing Shield Force's storage in your phone or browser settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you write to us with a question or a deletion request, we will confirm by email that we hold no data about you.

14. Third-party links

Screens such as Emergency and Contact link out to official government resources — cybercrime.gov.in, Sanchar Saathi, SPARSH and KSB — and to the national helpline via tel:1930. Those destinations are outside our control and have their own privacy practices.

15. Children's privacy

Shield Force is educational content aimed at adult service members, veterans and family members. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, so no age-specific handling is required — nothing is collected from any user regardless of age.

16. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 give you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you. Shield Force holds none, but you are always welcome to ask us about it at the address below.

17. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example if a future version added a feature that handles data — this page will be updated before the change takes effect and the “Last updated” date above will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.

18. Contact

For privacy questions, data-deletion requests, or anything else, use our Contact page or email vihaanssuvarna@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Use & Disclaimer.
