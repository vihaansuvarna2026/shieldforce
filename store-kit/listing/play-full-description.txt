Shield Force is a free awareness and training app for the Indian Armed Forces community. It helps serving personnel, veterans, veer naris and their families spot the scams that target them and act fast if one gets through.

NOT A GOVERNMENT APP
Shield Force is an independent community initiative. It does not represent, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, the Government of India, the Ministry of Defence, the Indian Armed Forces or any government body. Content is for education only. Statistics and map incidents are training examples, not official data. Always confirm details with the official sources listed below.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Scam Scanner. Paste a suspicious SMS, WhatsApp or email and get an instant risk verdict with every red flag explained. It runs on your phone and your message is never uploaded.
- Scam Intelligence. Eight scam types that target defence families, explained step by step with ways to stay safe.
- Training Modules. Six short quizzes that teach you to recognise fraud.
- Defence Schemes. AGIF, DSOP, SPARSH, ECHS, AFBCWF, PMSS, AWWA and CSD explained, each with a link to its official source, along with the scams that copy them.
- Emergency Help. Call the 1930 cyber fraud helpline in one tap and follow six clear steps for the first hour after a fraud.
- Official Sources. A list inside the app linking every government website the app refers to.
- Threat Map, Fraud Anatomy, Detection Lab and printable PDF guides.

14 LANGUAGES
English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Odia, Malayalam, Punjabi, Assamese and Nepali.

WORKS OFFLINE
Everything works without internet. Only the PDF guides and official website links need a connection.

PRIVACY
No account, no tracking, no ads and no data collection.
